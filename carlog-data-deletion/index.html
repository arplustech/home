CarLog User Data Deletion

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This page explains how to delete your data from the CarLog mobile application by AR Plus.

Deleting data stored in the app

CarLog stores your vehicles, logs, expenses, notes, and reminders on your device. You can remove it in either of these ways:

  • Delete individual vehicles or entries inside the app.
  • Uninstall CarLog, or clear its storage in your device settings, to remove all app data at once.

Data held by us

CarLog does not require an account, and we do not store your personal data on our servers. There is nothing further for us to delete.

Need help or have a request?

If you believe we hold any data about you, email ar.plus.tech@gmail.com with the subject "CarLog data deletion request". We will respond and delete any such data within 30 days.
