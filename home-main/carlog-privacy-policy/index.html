CarLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AR Plus ("we", "us") handles information in the CarLog mobile application ("CarLog", "the app").

1. Information we collect

CarLog lets you keep records about your vehicles. The information you enter, such as:

  • Vehicle details (make, model, year, plate number)
  • Mileage, fuel, maintenance, and expense entries
  • Notes and reminders you create

This data is stored on your device. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or location.

2. How we use information

Information you enter is used only to provide the app's features, such as showing your logs, statistics, and reminders.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

4. Data storage and security

Your data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored by it. We take reasonable steps to protect the app, but no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

5. Children's privacy

CarLog is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

7. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email ar.plus.tech@gmail.com.
